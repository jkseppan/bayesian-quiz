Continuous Ranked Probability Score

Lower CRPS = better prediction.

CRPS(F,y)= ∫−∞∞ (F(x)−𝟙[x≥y]) 2  dx

F = your Gaussian CDF, y = the true answer

Pretty good μ = 40 1.46 4.75
Inaccurate μ = 30 11.44 7.46
